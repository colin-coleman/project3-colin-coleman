Cosmo

Astro

Meet Cosmo! This 2-year-old feline friend loves to eat—fabric, in particular, is his meal of choice. Despite a recent pica diagnosis, he lives life to the fullest (albeit on the dangerous side). You can usually find him chasing spring toys or lounging in his favorite window-facing hammock, plotting his next snack. Full of chaos and charm, Cosmo keeps everyone on their toes.

Introducing Astro! At just 4 months old, this little guy is fully expecting to be the center of attention wherever he goes. He has a full-time job making biscuits directly in the crevice of your neck at 2 a.m., just in case you forgot he exists. Whether he’s snuggling up or pestering his older brother, Astro thinks personal space is simply a suggestion.

Reason for our Picnic!

Astro and Cosmo thought it would be a great idea to go to a park and enjoy the Las Vegas Spring time! It's gonna be a great picnic where everyone can meet up and have a fun and relaxing time. If you have the time and are free on May 9th, please come and have a great time with your pets!
